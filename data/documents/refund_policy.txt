REFUND POLICY

Processing Time
Approved refunds are processed within 5-7 business days after the returned item is received and inspected.

Refund Method
Refunds are issued to the original payment method used at purchase. Cash refunds are not offered for card or UPI payments.

Partial Refunds
Partial refunds may be issued if an item is returned damaged, missing parts, or outside the standard return window but within a reasonable grace period, at the discretion of support staff.

Refund Approval Thresholds
Refunds under 5,000 may be approved directly by support staff. Refunds of 5,000 or more require manager or supervisor approval before processing, due to the higher financial impact.

Failed or Delayed Refunds
If a refund is not received within 10 business days of approval, customers should contact support with their order ID for investigation.
