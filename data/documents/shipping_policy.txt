SHIPPING POLICY

Standard Shipping
Standard delivery takes 5-7 business days from the order date. Shipping is free on orders above 1,000.

Expedited Shipping
Expedited shipping (2-3 business days) is available at checkout for an additional fee.

Delays
Orders may be delayed due to courier issues, weather disruptions, stock shortages, or incorrect address information. Customers can check order status at any time using their order ID.

International Shipping
We currently ship only within the country of operation. International shipping is not supported at this time.

Lost Packages
If a package shows as delivered but was not received, customers should contact support within 48 hours to file an investigation with the courier.
