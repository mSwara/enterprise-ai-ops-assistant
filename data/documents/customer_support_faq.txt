CUSTOMER SUPPORT FAQ

Q: How do I track my order?
A: You can track your order status using your order ID through the support chat or your account dashboard.

Q: Can I cancel an order after placing it?
A: Orders can be cancelled within 1 hour of placement if they have not yet shipped. Contact support immediately to request cancellation.

Q: What payment methods are accepted?
A: We accept credit/debit cards, UPI, and net banking.

Q: How do I contact support?
A: Support is available through chat, email, or by creating a support ticket describing your issue.

Q: My order shows delivered but I did not receive it. What do I do?
A: Please contact support within 48 hours so we can investigate with our courier partner.
