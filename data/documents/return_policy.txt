RETURN POLICY

General Returns
Most items purchased from our store can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund, provided the item is unused and in its original packaging.

Electronics
Electronics (including phones, laptops, headphones, and accessories) may be returned within 15 days of delivery. The item must be in original condition with all accessories, cables, and packaging included. A 10% restocking fee applies to opened electronics unless the item is defective.

Clothing and Apparel
Clothing may be returned within 30 days if unworn, unwashed, and with original tags attached.

Non-Returnable Items
Gift cards, downloadable software, and personalized/customized items cannot be returned.

How to Initiate a Return
Customers should contact support with their order number to begin a return. Once approved, a prepaid return label will be issued.
