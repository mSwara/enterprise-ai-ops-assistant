PRODUCT POLICY

Warranty
Electronics come with a manufacturer warranty of 6-12 months depending on the product category. Warranty claims should be directed to the manufacturer unless the defect is discovered within the return window, in which case standard return policy applies.

Product Condition Guarantee
All products are quality-checked before shipping. Any product received damaged or defective should be reported within 7 days of delivery for a replacement or refund.

Counterfeit Policy
We guarantee all products sold are genuine. Any suspected counterfeit item should be reported to support immediately for investigation.
